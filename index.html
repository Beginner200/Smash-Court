Scoring: each completed rally awards one point. Games are first to 21 with a two-point lead, capped at 30; the match is best of three. Serve starts each rally from the score-selected diagonal service court. The rally winner serves next; AI serves automatically when it is the AI’s turn. Service height and landing box are checked by the Phase 1 rules engine. Player returns require racket-range contact with the moving shuttle; AI difficulty changes opponent movement speed, interception timing, reach, and (on Hard) shot placement. AI movement and shot selection remain prototype decisions, not full human-like badminton strategy. Physics uses fixed 1/240-second steps for deterministic replay; aerodynamic values are initial tuning estimates, not laboratory-calibrated data. Court line decisions reuse the Phase 1 rules module.
